Motor-Imagery EEG Classification

Classifying imagined movements from raw EEG. PSD-driven channel selection, wavelet features across seven bands, XGBoost with subject-specific training.

The pipeline

The full signal flow, animated end-to-end.

1
Participants & unipolar recording
Multi-electrode scalp EEG is recorded as unipolar channels referenced to a common electrode. Each trace captures a mix of cortical activity, reference noise, and volume conduction.
2
Unipolar → bipolar conversion
Adjacent electrodes are pairwise subtracted to form bipolar derivations, e.g. C3 − Cz. This cancels the shared reference noise and sharpens local cortical signal.
3
PSD per channel pair
Welch's method computes the power spectral density of every bipolar pair across the canonical bands — δ, θ, α, β (low/high), γ (low/high) — giving a 7-band energy profile per pair.
4
Top-10 pair selection
Pairs are ranked by discriminative power (class-conditional band-power separation), and the 10 best are kept — cutting dimensionality while retaining sensorimotor-rich channels.
5
Wavelet feature extraction
Discrete wavelet decomposition on each retained pair, harvesting per-band coefficients (means, energies, standard deviations) — giving a compact time-frequency feature vector.
6
XGBoost classification
A gradient-boosted tree ensemble classifies each trial into its imagined-movement class. Training is subject-specific — one model per participant, tuned on their own trials.
