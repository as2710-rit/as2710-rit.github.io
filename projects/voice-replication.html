Deep Learning · Audio

Voice Replication (XTTS v2)

High-fidelity neural voice cloning on consumer GPU hardware, gated by speaker verification so the clone answers only to its owner.

The approach

1
Dataset prep
Clean mono WAV clips of the target speaker, loudness-normalized, silence-trimmed, with aligned transcripts.
2
Fine-tune Coqui XTTS v2
GPT-based multilingual TTS conditioned on a speaker embedding — fine-tuned on the prepared clips so the model locks onto the target voice.
3
Speaker verification gate
Before synthesis, an MFCC d-vector verifier compares the requester's voice to the enrolled embedding. Only cosine similarity above threshold allows generation — the clone answers only to its owner.
4
Whisper grounding
Whisper transcribes incoming audio requests, keeping the whole loop text-tractable and auditable.
5
Serving
Realtime synthesis served behind a verification gateway, running on a single RTX-class GPU.
