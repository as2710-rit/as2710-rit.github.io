Identifying Pre-Seizure Interval Using Brain Activity

IEEE New York Joint Chapters Meeting (JCM) 2026 · Rochester, NY

I presented my research poster at the IEEE New York Joint Chapters Meeting (JCM) 2026 at the RIT Inn on October 27th, sharing my work on deriving a data-driven characterization of brain activity preceding epileptic events.

The presentation sparked engaging discussions — particularly around how AI models can assist clinicians in seizure prediction, surgery planning, and medication adjustment.

Through this work, I'm continuing to advance the frontier of smart healthcare, translating research into real-world solutions.

4 / 5

EEG-derived features identified the pre-seizure state in four of five tested time windows — with up to 73.3% accuracy.
