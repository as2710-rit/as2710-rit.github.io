About

I'm a PhD student at the Golisano College of Computing and Information Science, Rochester Institute of Technology, focused on applied machine learning for neurological disorders such as epilepsy. Apart from my main research, I work on a variety of ML projects from motor-imagery EEG classification to neural voice cloning. I am learning to ship working systems more than slide decks.

When I'm not training models, I'm building the tooling around them: pipelines, evaluation harnesses, and the occasional P2P network.

Selected Projects

Research · IEEE

Motor-Imagery EEG Classification →

Classifying imagined limb movements from raw EEG. The pipeline selects channels via power-spectral-density analysis, extracts Wavelet features across seven frequency bands, and trains an XGBoost classifier with subject-specific training — built toward an IEEE-style publication on BCIs.

PythonPSDWavelet XGBoostMOABB
Deep Learning · Audio

Voice Replication (XTTS v2) →

Fine-tuned Coqui's XTTS v2 for high-fidelity voice cloning on consumer GPU hardware. Covers dataset prep, speaker-encoder verification, and a serving setup for realtime synthesis — including a speaker-verification gate (MFCC d-vectors) so the clone answers only to its owner.
