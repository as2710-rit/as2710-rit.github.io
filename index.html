About

I'm a first-year PhD student in Computing and Information Sciences at the Rochester Institute of Technology, with a background in biomedical engineering from IIT (BHU) Varanasi. My research applies artificial intelligence to neurological signals — seizure prediction from EEG, brain–computer interfaces, and neural signal processing — toward monitoring, diagnosis, and rehabilitation.

Across internships at the Max Planck Institute, Fidelity, University of Alberta, and KU Leuven, I've worked the full stack of a research problem: acquiring neural data, building pipelines, and deploying models that hold up in the real world.
